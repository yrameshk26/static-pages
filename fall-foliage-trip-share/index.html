October long weekend · Fri to Mon

Fall Foliage Road Trip

Ajax to New Hampshire

A four-day drive from the Toronto area into the White Mountains, with two young kids. The route we drove, the hotels we used, and the stops worth building a day around.

The route at a glance

Fri Oct 9 · about 3 hours · 1 night

Ajax to Watertown, NY

Leave after 6 PM, cross at the Thousand Islands Bridge, and take a late hotel just over the border so Saturday is not a monster drive.

Sat Oct 10 · about 5.5 hours · 1 night

Watertown to Gilford, NH

Through the Adirondacks and the Lake Placid area, into New Hampshire on I-93, with Flume Gorge as the afternoon stop, then the Lakes Region for the night.

Sun Oct 11 · about 3 hours of driving, plus stops · 1 night

Gilford to North Conway, via Franconia Notch and the Kancamagus

The foliage day: Artist's Bluff if you start early, the Kancamagus waterfalls, a short scenic train in North Conway, then Cathedral Ledge and, if the kids still have energy, Diana's Baths.

Mon Oct 12 · 8 to 9 hours · home

North Conway back to Ajax

West on I-87, with a stretch or lunch near Lake George or Saratoga Springs, then the Thousand Islands Bridge home. No hotel at the end of this day.

Where we stayed

Nest Inn

1 night

Watertown, NY

A late-arrival stop just over the border, not a destination. Call ahead and tell them you will be in around 9:30 to 10:00 PM. We used a deluxe single with a king bed.

TownePlace Suites Laconia Gilford

1 night

Gilford, NH, Lakes Region

A good mid-trip base: free breakfast, and you are in position for an early Sunday run up I-93 into Franconia Notch. This is the hotel that makes the Kancamagus day possible without a pre-dawn start from New York State.

Eastern Inn & Suites

1 night

North Conway, NH

Right on White Mountain Highway, close to the village, the depot and the last two stops of the day. A standard king room was enough for one night. Book a dinner table in the village, because foliage-weekend walk-in waits can run past two hours.

Highlights and what we would recommend

  • Flume Gorge, Franconia Notch

    The Saturday afternoon stop, and worth booking a timed entry. We aimed at the 2:00 PM window. Leave the stroller behind: this one needs a baby carrier. It is about 2 miles of stairs, steep inclines and boardwalks.

  • The Kancamagus Highway, eastbound

    The heart of the Sunday drive, 34.5 miles with no gas and no food, and the phone signal drops out in stretches. Fill the tank and pack lunch in Lincoln. Hancock Overlook and Sugar Hill Overlook are quick roadside pull-offs. The developed falls lots charge a day-use fee, so have cash or a federal pass ready.

    Sabbaday Falls is the easiest waterfall, a wide, gradual path, about half an hour, though a stroller is only so-so, so keep a carrier handy. Rocky Gorge has a paved walk to the footbridge and is easy with a stroller. Do not jump or swim in the gorge. Lower Falls is wide granite ledges, not for a stroller, and slippery, so hold hands. No restrooms there, and the site closes for the season in mid-October. The Albany Covered Bridge is a quick extra if everyone is still game. Leave Lower Falls by about 1:15 if you have a 2:00 PM train to catch.

  • Valley Train from North Conway Depot

    We took the Observation Dome class, 2:30 to 3:25 PM, and would arrive by 2:00 because village traffic backs up in foliage season. Fold the stroller or leave it on the platform, the aisles are tight. The depot is the 1874 Victorian station at 38 Norcross Circle.

    If you have more time than we did, other trains to look at: the Conway Scenic Mountaineer, 4 to 5 hours through Crawford Notch, is the classic long foliage ride. The Granite State Railway, from Lincoln or Meredith, is a gentler, family-friendly run along the river or Lake Winnipesaukee. The Cafe Lafayette Dinner Train out of North Woodstock is a restored 1950s car with a dome and a five-course meal. Book any of these early.

  • Artist's Bluff, Cathedral Ledge and Diana's Baths

    Artist's Bluff is a 1.5 mile loop, steep at the start, carrier only, about an hour and a half with a young child. Skip it if you are not at the trailhead by about 9:30, because the Kancamagus and the train matter more. Park in the dirt lot across Route 18 from the Cannon base, not at Echo Lake. As of last autumn the state was running it as a one-way loop, counterclockwise.

    Cathedral Ledge has a road to the top, so it works with little ones, and the view over North Conway is the point. Check the gate time at the bottom, one listing has it closing at 6 PM. No facilities at the summit, about half an hour. Diana's Baths is 0.6 miles each way to granite cascades. The first stretch is packed gravel, then it gets rougher, so use the carrier. Skip it if the kids are done and go to dinner.

  • A stroller-friendly stretch in Lake Placid

    On the Saturday drive through the Adirondacks, Mirror Lake has a paved path if you want to get out and walk. North Conway village is also easy with a stroller, good sidewalks for a wander and a coffee after the train.

Sunday as one mapped route, Gilford to the depot to the hotel: open it in Google Maps. Check the pins land where you expect before you set off.

Food picks

  • Pack lunch for the Kancamagus. There is nowhere to buy food along that stretch. Pick it up in Lincoln with the fuel stop.
  • Book dinner in North Conway or Lincoln. On a foliage weekend, walk-in waits can exceed two hours. We ate in North Conway village after the last lookout.
  • Tanger Outlets in Tilton, optional. About 20 minutes south of Gilford, open until 8 on a Saturday. New Hampshire has no sales tax. Doing it on Saturday keeps Sunday free for the leaves.
  • Monday lunch. A stretch stop near Lake George or Saratoga Springs, NY, breaks up the long drive home.

Practical tips

  • Bring a carrier, not just a stroller. The stroller is great in North Conway, at the Kancamagus pull-offs, and at Rocky Gorge. Leave it behind for Flume Gorge, Artist's Bluff, Lower Falls, Diana's Baths, and the train.
  • Book early. Flume Gorge timed entry, the scenic train, and the three hotels all go in October. Dinner reservations in North Conway or Lincoln are almost as important.
  • Timing on Sunday is tight. The bluff only works if you leave Gilford around 7:30 AM. If you are late, skip it. The falls and a 2:00 PM depot arrival are the day.
  • The border on the way home. After more than 48 hours out of Canada, each traveller has a CAD $800 personal exemption, children included. The goods have to belong to the person claiming them, and they have to be in the car. Keep the receipts together and declare everything.
